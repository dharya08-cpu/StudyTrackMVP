StudyTrack Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026. StudyTrack is run by Dharya Goyel ("we"). Contact: santoshgoyel977@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • From Google sign-in: your name, Gmail address and Google account ID. We never see or store a password.
  • Study data you or your teachers enter: classes you join, study logs, homework submissions, test marks, attendance, syllabus progress, study goals, exam dates, weekly plans, announcements and notifications.
  • Files that teachers upload (PDF, JPG, PNG, DOCX).
  • Security data: for each signed-in device, the device type, operating system, browser, last-active time and a partly hidden network address (never the full address). Failed sign-in attempts are logged to protect accounts.

Why we use it

Only to run StudyTrack: to show your progress, connect you with your classes, send class notifications and keep accounts secure. We do not sell your data, show ads or track you across other websites.

Who can see it

  • You see your own data.
  • A teacher sees the data of the students in that teacher's classes. An institute head can see the classes of their institute.
  • The StudyTrack administrator can access data to provide support and keep the service safe.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase (database and file storage) and the app runs on Render. Google provides sign-in. Files are private and are shared only through short-lived links to members of the right class.

Cookies

StudyTrack uses only essential cookies: one keeps you signed in, and a short-lived security cookie is used while you sign in with Google. We use no advertising or analytics cookies. Google's sign-in button may set its own cookies, which are covered by Google's privacy policy.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept until you ask us to delete it. Sign-in sessions expire after at most 30 days. Notifications are deleted after about 120 days. We keep backups to recover from mistakes or outages: daily backups for about 2 weeks, weekly backups for about 8 weeks, and a small number of manual backups that may be kept longer. If you ask us to delete your data, it can remain in these backups until they are replaced by newer ones.

Your choices

You can leave a class, sign out of any device, or email us at santoshgoyel977@gmail.com to see, correct or delete your data. We will reply within 30 days.

Children

StudyTrack is used by students who may be under 18. Students under 18 should use it with the knowledge of a parent, guardian or their teacher. Teachers and institutes are responsible for having any permission they need from parents.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
